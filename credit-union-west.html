Transforming the Brand Experience to Support Growth

Credit Union West | Brand Transformation, Integrated Marketing, Business Growth & Community Engagement

13 Branches rebranded
107% Loan volume growth in Q1
45% Deposit revenue growth over six months
01 / The Challenge

Preparing a Growing Credit Union for Expansion

Credit Union West was preparing for expansion and needed a consistent brand experience across its branch network. Opportunities to strengthen brand recognition, attract new members, and increase deposits called for a coordinated marketing strategy.

The objective was to create a recognizable identity while aligning marketing initiatives with business growth and community engagement.

02 / The Strategy

Connecting Brand, Growth, and Community

As Marketing Director, I led a comprehensive marketing approach built around three priorities.

Brand Consistency

Establish a unified look and feel across 13 branches to strengthen recognition and create a consistent member experience.

Business Growth

Develop integrated campaigns focused on deposit growth, lending opportunities, and member acquisition.

Community Engagement

Increase brand visibility and strengthen relationships through community partnerships, sponsorships, and regional events.

03 / Leadership & Execution

Bringing the Brand to Life Across 13 Branches

I led the transformation of Credit Union West's physical and visual brand experience, overseeing exterior signage, interior branding, printed marketing materials, and promotional displays.

The initiative required coordinating marketing priorities and execution to deliver a cohesive customer-facing experience across the branch network.

Alongside the rebrand, I directed marketing initiatives focused on increasing deposits, supporting lending growth, and strengthening relationships with members.

04 / Community Impact

Building Relationships Beyond the Branch

Community engagement played an important role in our marketing strategy. I was actively involved in regional events and partnerships, including home shows, Paul's Car Wash benefiting Big Brothers Big Sisters, Luke Days at Luke Air Force Base, and other West Valley community events.

These activities connected Credit Union West with local audiences and supported its presence as a community-focused financial institution.

05 / Business Outcomes

Supporting Measurable Business Growth

During my marketing leadership tenure, Credit Union West achieved a 107% increase in loan volume in Q1 and 45% growth in deposit revenue over six months.

The 13-branch rebranding initiative also established a more consistent brand experience to support the credit union's expansion objectives.

This work reflects my approach to marketing leadership: aligning brand strategy, customer experience, integrated campaigns, and execution with broader business objectives.
