The challenge

Strengthen member acquisition, lending activity, and deposit growth while supporting the credit union’s wider expansion priorities.

My approach

  • Directed integrated marketing initiatives around deposit growth, lending opportunities, and member acquisition.
  • Aligned campaign planning and marketing resource allocation with business priorities.
  • Led a full-service marketing team and communicated plans and results to executive leadership and the board.

Business outcomes

During my tenure, Credit Union West reported 107% Q1 loan volume growth and 45% deposit revenue growth over six months. These are organizational results achieved alongside coordinated marketing and operational efforts.

The challenge

As the credit union prepared for expansion, its branches needed a more cohesive identity that members could recognize across locations.

My approach

  • Led brand consistency efforts across all 13 branches.
  • Oversaw exterior signage, interior branding, printed marketing materials, and promotional displays.
  • Coordinated marketing priorities and execution to create a cohesive customer-facing experience.

The result

A consistent physical and visual brand presence across the branch network, supporting recognition and a more unified member experience. No independent lift in brand awareness is claimed.

The challenge

Strengthen community visibility and relationships while ensuring sponsorships and outreach supported the institution’s broader marketing strategy.

My approach

  • Led community relations and sponsorship initiatives involving nonprofits, military stakeholders, and local community partners.
  • Supported regional events including home shows, Paul's Car Wash benefiting Big Brothers Big Sisters, and Luke Days at Luke Air Force Base.
  • Worked with community and local-government stakeholders, including partnerships involving the Mayor of Glendale.

The contribution

These programs helped Credit Union West connect with local audiences and reinforce its role as a community-focused financial institution. The source materials do not establish a separate numerical return for these partnerships.
