The Leadership Story

Turning strategy into experiences people can use.

At First American Title, I led and collaborated on marketing initiatives that connected technology innovation with customer needs, sales effectiveness, and employee adoption.

My work spanned go-to-market strategy for customer- and employee-facing technologies, leadership of an in-house creative team, and the development of resources to help employees incorporate AI into everyday workflows.

Across these initiatives, the common priority was alignment: bringing the right teams together, creating clear value propositions, and translating complex ideas into useful, engaging experiences.

01 / Go-to-Market Strategy

Building the strategy behind technology launches.

Developed go-to-market strategies for customer-facing and employee-facing technologies, aligning sales, product development, engineering, and other stakeholders around shared launch priorities.

For customer-facing solutions, our strategies addressed distinct audiences: B2B real estate agents and B2C homebuyers and sellers. We used competitive analysis, value proposition testing, and beta testing to refine messaging, understand customer needs, and inform launch execution.

02 / Creative Leadership & Sales Enablement

Creating marketing that equips teams and connects with customers.

Led an in-house creative team responsible for customer-facing websites, SEO strategy, social media, digital marketing assets, printed collateral, and sales enablement materials.

Our approach connected creative development with business objectives. Beyond design, the work included content strategy, messaging, and defining how materials would be used by their intended audiences.

For the 7P sales methodology, the underlying sales process was established by the business. My team's role was to translate that process into branded, practical materials that supported consistent communication and sales enablement.

03 / AI Transformation & Enablement

Making AI practical, accessible, and relevant.

Collaborated with my team to develop an interactive click-through experience that connected business-unit-specific workflows with ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts.

Rather than relying on generic AI messaging, the experience focused on helping employees recognize opportunities to use AI in their own work and giving them a practical starting point.

We also created quick-start guides, how-to resources, and supporting materials for an AI Champions group to help employees learn, experiment, and build confidence using AI tools.
